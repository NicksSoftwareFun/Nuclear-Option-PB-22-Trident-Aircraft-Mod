# Changelog

## 0.9.0
- Release candidate.
- Debug log lines are off by default (`Testing/DebugLog` in the config).

## 0.4.10
- Engine fans spin and the cockpit gauges work in flight.
- The AI no longer bounces back into the air after touching down.

## 0.4.9
- Bigger, readable livery text.
- Smoother shading along the panel seams.
- Level rear crew windows.

## 0.4.8
- Live cockpit gauges.

## 0.4.7
- Portholes in the pilot hatches.
- Softer panel lines, matte intake ducts.

## 0.4.6
- Material and intake gap fixes from the 0.4.5 test.

## 0.4.5
- New windscreen with a better view over the nose; pilots sit higher.
- New cockpit interior with working yokes and throttles.

## 0.4.4
- Lighter model (about 30% fewer triangles) with more internal structure.

## 0.4.3
- Markings painted into the livery texture, like the stock aircraft.

## 0.4.0
- Renamed to PB-22 Trident.
- Reshaped intakes with open ducts.
- Seam grime per livery: grey cleanest, Low-Level Camo dirtier, Maritime Blue with rust tones.

## 0.3.9
- Stronger jammer (about 20 s at full power).
- AI ground-roll and turning fixes.

## 0.3.0
- Maritime Blue and Low-Level Camo liveries.
- Internal structure for battle damage.
- New flight model.

## 0.2.0
- Integrated four-seat cockpit with ejection seats and roof hatches.
- Electronic warfare tail cone.
- Detailed wheel wells and bays.

## 0.1.0
- First flyable version.
