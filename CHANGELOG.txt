# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Damage now works like the stock aircraft. Parts break off along straight panel lines with no gaps at the joints, the skin can be seen from both sides, and torn metal edges come from the damage texture. A broken-off section shows the frames and the inside of the skin instead of a flat lid.
- Honeycomb core shows under torn skin, using the game's own honeycomb material.
- Tyres shred when the main gear pods are hit.
- Afterburner flame drawn at its intended length.
- Nose tip straightened. It sat 12 cm low and looked hooked.
- Structure no longer pokes through the skin behind the dorsal grilles or along the spine.
- Dorsal chaff dispensers restyled so they no longer read as holes.
- New Livery Bundle folder in the repository for painting your own liveries. The texture layout changed in this release, so liveries painted for 1.0.0 do not line up.

## 1.0.0
- Six liveries: BDF and PALA versions of Matte Grey, Maritime Blue and Low-Level Camo. Camo and grey carry full-colour faction roundels on the fin and wings; Maritime Blue gets low-vis ones.
- The "To Sink a Carrier" mission now comes with the mod and is listed with the built-in missions. No separate download.
- Rear crew no longer spin round on the ejection rail.
- Fixed flickering surfaces in and behind the cockpit when seen from outside.
- Inner spoilers stay drawn closed with the wings swept back.
- Tailplane: a fixed root section on the hull, and the moving part is drawn at 60% of its real travel so it no longer looks like it's flapping. Handling is unchanged.
- Rudder travel 15 to 11 degrees. It could yaw itself round too hard.
- Fuel 53,550 to 36,000 kg, closer to the other heavies on endurance.
- Softer yaw assist on the ground (3 to 2, the same as the CAS and COIN) to stop the shaking while taxiing. Full rudder still turns as tight as before.
- The landing log setting is now off by default.

## 0.9.4
- Rank 5 and cost 225, the same as the Darkreach.
- Main gear pods keep their flat tops under the wing, so the gap no longer shows into the gear bay.
- Closed the slot in the belly next to the weapon bays.
- Fixed the nacelle frame poking through the skin, gaps between the nozzle petals and the ends of the nose gear doors.
- Canopy frames: a raised border painted around the outside in every livery, and a dark frame along every glass edge inside.
- Cockpit: solid centre console, rudder pedals slide in floor channels, and nothing clips through the glass any more.

## 0.9.3
- Rudder pedals now slide in and out with the foot plates kept square, instead of tilting.
- Closed a hole in the top of the main gear fairing at its outboard front corner, ahead of the wing glove.

## 0.9.2
- Fixed AI aircraft lifting off again right after touchdown and aborting the landing (the touchdown and takeoff pitch helpers pushed the wrong way).
- Engine status text on the centre MFD now sits inside the engine box.

## 0.9.1
- AI landings no longer abort on a small bounce.
- A PB-22 that finishes its landing roll leaves the runway queue, so following aircraft don't go around.
- Radar cross-section 0.08 (was 0.0025).
- AI loadouts trimmed to 8, with three new ones: Static, Gauntlet and Broadside (the new default).
- Pilot porthole gap closed, with a clean frame ring.
- EXT PWR stencil no longer repeated inside the intake.
- Hatch warning stripes no longer flicker at the corners.
- Optional landing log (`Testing/LandingLog` in the config).

## 0.9.0
- Tac screen camera feeds show their full width again, so the RNG/ALT/HDG labels are no longer cut off.
- Removed the unused standby horizon from the tac screen.
- Plugin diagnostic log lines are off by default (`Testing/DebugLog` in the config).
