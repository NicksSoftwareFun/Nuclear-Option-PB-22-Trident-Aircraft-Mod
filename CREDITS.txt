# Credits

## Game

Nuclear Option and its assets are the property of Shockfront Studios. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Shockfront Studios.

The PB-22 Trident is built on the stock Alkyon AB-4 setup. Its exterior model, cockpit and textures are original, made to match the look of the game's own aircraft. At load time Blueprinter points the bundle at the game's own shaders, effects, sounds, weapons and pylons.

The bundle also carries a few copies of stock game assets as named PB-22 parts: the internal structure textures (seen through battle damage) and the Medusa ejection seats. They remain the property of Shockfront Studios. This repository does not contain them.
