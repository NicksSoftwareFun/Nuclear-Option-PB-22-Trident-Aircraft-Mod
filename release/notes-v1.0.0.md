The first full release of the PB-22 Trident.

![PB-22 Trident on the ramp](https://github.com/NicksSoftwareFun/nuclear-option-pb-22-trident-aircraft-mod/raw/main/docs/images/2_ramp.png)

The Trident is a long-range naval strike bomber, loosely based on the Tu-22M3 Backfire. It isn't meant to be a one-for-one copy of the old Soviet jet. It's more of a modern take on the idea, and it slots in between the Medusa and the Darkreach. You get swing wings, two big afterburning engines, a crew of four and enough fuel to get a long way out over the water and back. Wings forward for takeoff and cruising, swept back when it's time to go fast.

Its main job is sinking ships. It has a forward bomb bay, a rear bomb bay, two glove pylons and two wing pylons, plus a built-in jammer for when the SAMs come up.

### Preset loadouts

- **Broadside** (default): 12 AGM-99 in the bays, 4 Tusko-B on the gloves, 6 GPO-500 on the wings
- **Sea Lance**: 16 AShM-300, half inside and half on the pylons
- **Needle**: 16 Tusko-B, same split
- **Carpet**: 30 PAB-250 from the bays and 12 GPO-500 on the pylons
- **Doomsday**: 2 ALND-4 (20kt) cruise missiles inside, 2 Piledriver TBM (20kt) on the gloves and 2 conventional Piledrivers on the wings
- **Wild Weasel**: 8 Tusko-B inside, 8 ARAD-116 outside
- **Static**: 8 Tusko-B inside, 6 GPO-500 on the gloves and two jamming pods on the wings
- **Gauntlet**: 12 GPO-500 inside, 4 ARAD-116 and 4 AAM-29 Scythe for self defence

There are six liveries: Matte Grey, Maritime Blue and Low-Level Camo, each in a BDF and a PALA version. The mod also comes with a mission built around the Trident, **To Sink a Carrier**, which shows up with the built-in missions.

### Install

You need BepInEx 5 and Blueprinter 2.0.1 or newer. Drop `PB22Trident.dll` into `Nuclear Option\BepInEx\plugins` and start the game. That's the whole mod, one file.

If you put the mission in your Missions folder by hand for 0.9.4, delete that copy or you'll see it listed twice.

The full list of changes since 0.9.4 is in [CHANGELOG.txt](https://github.com/NicksSoftwareFun/nuclear-option-pb-22-trident-aircraft-mod/blob/main/CHANGELOG.txt).
