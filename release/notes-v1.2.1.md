The PB-22 can now carry weapons from the Aryx Weaponry Pack and Boscali Summer, and it shakes less around Mach 1.

### Weapon compatibility

When the Aryx Weaponry Pack or Boscali Summer is installed, its weapons show up in the PB-22's loadout menu. Neither mod is required. The PB-22 works with one, both or neither, and anything from a mod you don't have simply isn't offered.

- Forward or rear bay: PAB-3000LR x2 and the AGM-76 Atlatl rotary rack (Aryx).
- Combined bay: HSM-750 Starfall, PGM-400LR x8 (Aryx); HAWC-X x2, GLAIVE-2A x2 and GLAIVE-2S x2 (Boscali).
- Glove and wing pylons: AGM-111 Nemesis, PGM-400LR x2, Tusko-N (Aryx); AGT-80 ORCA x1 and x3, XQ-58V REMORA, HAWC-X (Boscali).
- Wing pylons only: 2000 kg drop tank (Aryx). The Starfall is also offered on the wing pylons, but Aryx marks that version as event content, so it only appears in missions with event content turned on.

### Shake fix

The nose and cockpit are now braced to the forward fuselage the same way the stock AB-4's are, so the long nose no longer shakes the cockpit, and the camera with it, through the transonic range.

### Install

You need BepInEx 5 and Blueprinter 2.0.1 or newer. Drop `PB22Trident.dll` into `Nuclear Option\BepInEx\plugins`, replacing the old one, and start the game.
